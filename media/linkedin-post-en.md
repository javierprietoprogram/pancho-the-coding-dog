I had a problem. So I built a dog. 🐶

My day looks like this: Claude Code in one window, Codex in another, and each of them spinning up its own subagents. Three, four, sometimes more agents working at the same time.

And I was the bottleneck.

One of them would stop to ask me something and I wouldn't notice. I'd find out 20 minutes later, after jumping window by window trying to work out who needed me, who was still working and who had already finished.

So I fixed it for myself, with Pancho.

Why Pancho? Because he's my dog, I love him to bits, and I liked the idea of having him keep an eye on my agents while I work.

What it does:
→ Floats in a corner of my Mac and shows every Claude Code and Codex conversation at a glance.
→ Tells me the moment one of them has a question or needs permission, and I answer right there, without switching windows.
→ New: one panel with every conversation and the subagents it launched, live.
→ Finished chats turn green, and one click takes me straight back to them.

In short: all my agents and subagents managed from one place, instead of going window by window.

I'm leaving it here for free and open source. Copy it, use it, and if you improve it, even better. Pull requests are very welcome.

No accounts, no API keys, and it never stores your prompts. Everything stays on your Mac.

👉 https://github.com/javierprietoprogram/pancho-the-coding-dog
